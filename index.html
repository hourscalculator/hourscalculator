    Overtime explained

    Over 40 hours a week (default)
    Hours above 40 in a workweek are paid at 1.5×. Federal standard, used in most states.
    Over 8 hours a day
    Hours above 8 in a workday are paid at 1.5×. Used for daily overtime, including in states such as California.
    California rules
    Hours above 8 through 12 in a workday are paid at 1.5×. Hours above 12 are paid at 2×. On the seventh consecutive workday, the first 8 hours are paid at 1.5× and additional hours at 2×. Weekly overtime is included without counting premiums twice.

    About this time card calculator

    Time Card Calculator helps you turn everyday clock entries into a clear record of hours and estimated earnings. Start with your first arrival and departure, then add any additional work periods or breaks. Your weekly totals update as you enter times, with separate figures for regular hours, overtime, and double time when applicable. Choose a single week, several weeks, or a calendar month to match your reporting routine. You can adjust the workweek boundary, review overnight shifts, and compare decimal hours with familiar hours and minutes. Keep a draft in your browser, download a detailed CSV, or print a readable copy. Share a snapshot when someone else needs to review the same entries before you submit your final time card.

    How to use the time card calculator

    1. Choose a date and the first day of your workweek.
    2. Enter In and Out times. Try 855 for 8:55, 9 for 9:00, or 930p for 9:30 PM. An explicit a/p suffix takes priority. Otherwise the visible AM/PM selection applies. With AM/PM hidden, use 24 hour times.
    3. Add work periods and actual break start and end times. Mark paid breaks when appropriate.
    4. Use Settings to choose the reporting period, hourly rate and overtime rule. Review context days around month boundaries.
    5. Review the automatically saved results, then print, export CSV, or copy a report link.

    The timeline is an additional editor. Focus a marked endpoint and use its arrow keys to adjust it in five minute steps, or enter exact times in the always visible fields. Out times earlier than In continue into the next day.

    How we calculate

    Worked minutes = work period minutes − unpaid break minutes. Paid breaks remain within worked time. Gaps between separate work periods are already excluded. Automatic daily unpaid break adds an editable break starting at noon to each day. Editing a break turns off the automatic option.

    Weekly overtime uses hours above 40 in each separate workweek. Daily mode uses hours above 8 per workday. California standard mode uses 1.5× after 8 through 12 hours, 2× after 12, and seventh consecutive workday rules, with weekly overtime added without duplicating daily premiums. Estimated gross pay uses actual minutes and the selected hourly rate, rounded once to cents for the report.

    Civil dates and clock minutes are used without device timezone conversion. Daylight saving transitions are not automatically adjusted. Special industry rules, alternative workweeks, bonuses, multiple rates, taxes and meal penalties require separate handling. Missing adjacent workday information is marked as an incomplete estimate.

    1. Decimal hours are converted from each actual hh:mm total, rather than summed from rounded daily decimal figures.
    2. The minutes after the colon in hh:mm are base 60. For example, 7:30 means seven and one half hours.
    3. Decimal hours and report display rounding preserve actual minutes and estimated pay. Three days of 8:01 total 24:03, displayed as 24.05 decimal hours.

    Rule references U.S. Department of Labor and California Labor Commissioner.

    Frequently asked questions

    How do I subtract a lunch break?

    Add its actual start and end times as an unpaid break within the work period. Alternatively enable the editable automatic break setting. Automatic daily breaks start at noon. Adjust their input fields or use the arrow keys on their green timeline endpoints.

    Can I enter a shift that crosses midnight?

    Yes. An Out time earlier than In continues into the next day. Work and breaks are split at your chosen workday boundary. Equal times mean zero duration.

    Can I calculate a biweekly time card?

    Choose 2 weeks in Settings. Each workweek keeps its own 40 hour overtime threshold, so two week totals are never averaged.

    What does California mode include?

    It includes standard daily, weekly, double time and seventh consecutive workday premiums. Special exemptions, alternative workweeks and meal penalties are outside this calculator.

    Why might decimal hours differ from payroll?

    Your payroll system may apply different rounding or compensation policies. Decimal display rounding here does not change actual minutes or estimated gross pay.

    Will my time card be saved?

    The calculator saves drafts in this browser's local storage when available. Check the save status. Browser cleanup or private browsing can remove a draft.

    How can I print or save a PDF?

    Choose Print / PDF after correcting input errors, then use your browser's print dialog to select a printer or Save as PDF.

    Where is my data stored and who can read a shared link?

    Drafts stay in browser storage. Sharing deliberately places a compressed snapshot in the link fragment. Anyone holding that link can read it. Snapshots do not sync and cannot be revoked.

    Time card calculator checks before payroll

    Review the reporting dates before sending a time card. A correct total attached to the wrong period can still delay payroll or an invoice. Set the first day of the workweek, check the first and last dates shown in the report, and compare the entries with your own work record. For a two week card, review each week separately before checking the combined total. Weekly overtime depends on the hours in each workweek.

    Check clock entries in pairs. Arrival and departure times should describe the same work period, including shifts that continue past midnight. Review the AM and PM selections when using twelve hour time. If a total looks unexpectedly large, inspect those selections before changing the hours. Add separate work periods when you leave and return during the day, and record breaks at their actual times.

    Time card calculator for client invoices

    Freelancers can add a client name and project name to each time card. Use a separate card when you need a separate record for another client or project. These labels travel with the saved card and appear in CSV and printed reports, helping the recipient match the hours to the right assignment. Add daily content when a client needs a short explanation of the work performed.

    Before attaching a report to an invoice, check the hourly rate, currency and billing mode. Estimated gross pay reflects the calculator settings and entered time. Compare that amount with the agreed billing terms. Export the CSV for a spreadsheet review or use Print / PDF to save a readable report with the client and project details. Keep a downloaded copy with your invoice records.

    Compare time card calculator totals with your records

    When a payroll total differs, start with one day rather than adjusting the final amount. Compare the arrival time, departure time and each break interval with the source record. Check whether a break was paid and whether an automatic interval still needs editing. After the daily minutes agree, compare the week containing that day. This helps identify whether the difference comes from entered time, the reporting boundary or overtime settings.

    Decimal display can introduce another source of confusion. Thirty minutes is half an hour, so seven hours and thirty minutes appears as 7.50 hours. A display of 7.30 represents a different duration. The report keeps hours and minutes alongside decimal hours so you can compare both representations. Changing display precision preserves the minutes used in calculations. Check the total converted from minutes when rounded daily values appear to add up differently.

    For shared work, confirm the client and project labels before copying a snapshot link. A snapshot captures the current entries for the recipient to review. If you later edit a draft, send a fresh link when the recipient needs those changes. Browser storage keeps the working draft on that browser, so download a CSV or PDF when you need a record outside the current device.
